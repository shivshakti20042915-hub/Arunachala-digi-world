Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How long does it take to build a website?+

Most websites are completed in 5–14 business days depending on complexity. Simple landing pages can be done in 3–5 days, while full e-commerce sites take 2–4 weeks.

What is your pricing model?+

We believe in affordable, transparent pricing. Every project is quoted based on scope. We offer packages for every budget — from startups to established businesses. Contact us for a free quote.

Do you provide ongoing support after project delivery?+

Absolutely. We offer dedicated post-launch support for all clients. This includes bug fixes, minor updates, and performance monitoring. Long-term maintenance plans are also available.

Can you work with businesses outside your local area?+

Yes! We work with clients across India and internationally. All communication is done online via WhatsApp, email, and video calls — making distance completely irrelevant.

What information do I need to get started?+

Just your business name, goals, and a rough idea of what you need. We'll take it from there during your free consultation and guide you through the entire process step by step.
